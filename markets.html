03 / FROM OBSERVATION TO EVIDENCE

A book view is a starting point.
A benchmark needs telemetry.

01

Match the experiment

Compare the same market, time window, capital, inventory limits, and risk constraints. Public account grouping establishes attribution, not algorithmic advantage.

02

Measure the full decision loop

End-to-end decision latency must include market data, classical preprocessing, quantum execution, and order submission. Requires instrumented strategy telemetry.

03

Evaluate execution and outcomes

Net P&L after fees, inventory exposure, fill rate, adverse selection, and risk-adjusted returns require reconciled fills and strategy records. No performance or speedup is inferred here.
